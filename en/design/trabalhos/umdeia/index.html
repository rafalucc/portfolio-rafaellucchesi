10 years leading a Design Studio

2019—2009

bh/mg

mission

Build a Design studio capable of sustaining itself and growing for more than five years without relying on a single industry to survive.

problem

The team's delivery capacity wasn't keeping pace with the volume of projects. Every new client increased the risk of overload and declining quality, which meant rethinking how the team operated—not simply adding more people.

learnings

  • O valor do Design está em resolver problems de negócio;
  • A Design business also needs to be designed;
  • Knowledge needs to become process in order to scale;
  • There is no good Design without business understanding;
  • Small teams demand clarity and collaboration;
  • Relationships matter as much as the portfolio;
  • Relevance is sustained by the ability to adapt.

challenge

How might we scale without compromising the quality of our project delivery?

results

10+ years operating in strategic Design;

50+ companies served across Brazil;

10+ professionals led throughout the decade.
