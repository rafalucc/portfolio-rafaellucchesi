mercantil do brasil

APP: Digital Account Opening for 100K+ Customers

2019—2018

bh/mg

mission

I worked as Senior Product Designer on Banco Mercantil’s digital account-opening app. The mission was to turn business requirements into an intuitive mobile experience.

problem

The account opening journey combined multiple steps, business rules, banking requirements, and validations, creating a complex, time-consuming experience with a high information load for users.

learnings

  • Design works as a bridge between business and technology;
  • Understanding the market improves product decisions;
  • Não comece pelas telas. Comece pelo problem;
  • A list of requirements is not yet an experience;
  • Business complexity doesn't need to reach the user;
  • Prototyping reduces the gap between intention and experience;
  • Aligning before designing reduces rework.

challenge

How might we turn multiple banking requirements into a simple journey that makes sense to the person on the other side of the app?

results

1 end-to-end journey structured;

100% of the flow converted into a navigable UX;

100K+ accounts opened through the app.
