90% CSAT: simplifying access management

2026—2023

bh/mg

mission

Transform Hotmart’s Collaborator Center into a simpler, more intuitive, and more consistent experience for creators who manage teams in their businesses.

problem

Users struggled to find the Collaborator Center, understand its purpose, and configure permissions because the screens were complex and inconsistent.

learnings

  • A deeper understanding of a feature leads to more effective solutions;
  • Listening to users with empathy reveals opportunities beyond the interface;
  • Usability problems may stem from product structure rather than the UI;
  • Standardizing permissions helps reduce ambiguity and prevent errors;
  • A shared language for permissions connects users with product rules;
  • Usability and satisfaction metrics measure the effectiveness of a redesign;
  • Delivering a new version requires alignment across Design, Product, and Engineering.

challenge

How might we make the Collaborator Center easier to find, clarify its purpose, and simplify permissions management across the platform?

results

50% reduction in difficulty finding the Collaborator Center;

100 fewer customer support tickets per month;

90% customer satisfaction (CSAT) among interviewees.

team members

Maycon Costa, Alencar Azevedo, Felipe Sena, Sandro Pimenta e Rafael Lucchesi :)
