mission

Realizei uma consultoria de Branding & Design, com a mission de construir um ecossistema de marcas que englobe o portfolio de produtos do Grupo Conor.

problem

Três pesquisas com 27 clientes revelaram que o problem não era comunicação de portfólio, mas de posicionamento, clientes viam o grupo como fornecedor de rastreamento, não como parceiro de crescimento, com até 100% de desconhecimento sobre produtos do ecossistema.

learnings

  • Branding starts with the business, not the visual identity;
  • Research improves the quality of decisions;
  • Customer perception matters more than our own opinion;
  • Consistency is built through governance, not just documentation;
  • Repositioning only happens when everyone takes part;
  • A strong brand communicates benefits before features;
  • Brand architecture is a growth tool.

challenge

How might we create an integrated multibrand ecosystem of solutions that helps customers grow in a practical, sustainable, and scalable way?

results

100% of departments aligned with the new brand strategy;

9.3 average NPS score after the branding plan;

3+ initiatives generated per month, aligned with the culture.
