getrak

Brand & UI/UX are differentiators for 57% of B2B customers

2018—2010

bh/mg

mission

Entre 2010 e 2018, prestei consultoria de Branding & Design para a Getrak, com a mission de torná-la referência em tecnologia de rastreamento veicular no mercado nacional.

problem

O problem estava na abordagem corporativa relacionada ao Design antes de 2010. A antiga marca tinha pouca diferenciação em relação aos concorrentes e não transmitia os atributos que o produto entregava aos clientes.

learnings

  • Before redesigning the brand, you need to understand the product;
  • A B2B brand also needs to build differentiation;
  • Branding needs to reflect the business strategy;
  • The digital product is also a brand touchpoint;
  • Identity and experience need to be connected;
  • Strategic Design goes beyond creating interfaces;
  • Bringing Design into strategic decisions is good business.

challenge

How might we differentiate the brand from competitors and align it with the real attributes delivered by the product?

results

120+ new B2B customers after the brand and product launch;

150,000+ new tracked vehicles (2011–2012);

57% of customers identified UI/UX as a differentiator.
