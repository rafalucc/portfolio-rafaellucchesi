Design System Scaled across 30+ Teams

mission

I joined Hotmart in 2023 as part of the Cosmos DS team, with the mission of restructuring the company's Design System alongside two other designers.

problem

The problem was the lack of reusable product patterns connecting the Design System to the real needs of product teams.

learnings

  • The Design System needs to be readable by both people and generative agents;
  • UI consistency starts with tokens, assets, and components;
  • Interface patterns make design decisions reusable;
  • Governance is part of the system experience;
  • Scaling means reducing repetitive software work;
  • Product consistency starts with standardization;
  • AI depends on a well-structured context foundation.

challenge

How might we turn Cosmos into a design platform capable of reducing inconsistencies across products and increasing team efficiency?

results

30+ teams involved in the adoption program;

99+ active internal users;

8.7 average satisfaction score for product patterns.
