    An app that brings home and family care together in one place: sensors, residents, and assistance one tap away. I designed the screens in 2017, during Umdeia’s consulting work for Getrak, as a proposal presented to the French company Mondial Assistance. I have now rebuilt the flow in code to show the complete experience in motion, from login to emergency assistance on the way.
