Descriptive Memorial

It all begins with the challenge of creating art capable of communicating with the subconscious. To make this possible, a set of principles was written into a manifesto. Its purpose is to keep sight of what truly matters in a society increasingly distracted by superficiality.

Written in May 2023, the V. O. A. Manifesto is a commitment to personal growth and a foundation for building a culture of development. Three values emerge from it:

  1. Depth over superficiality;
  2. The continuous development of skills over the affirmation of qualities;
  3. The desire to learn over recognition of talent.

Turn Inward, Observe Yourself, and Learn. The answer was to turn the title into a path. The gallery is divided into three spaces measuring 10.84 m, following the sequence V. O. A., with each space representing a stage of the journey.

  • V. — Turn Inward: Look within. Slow down, pause, and acknowledge feelings, thoughts, and emotions without judgment;
  • O. — Observe Yourself: Look at the world. Notice how our actions affect those around us, with empathy, respect, and equity;
  • A. — Learn: The path of transformation. Recognize that no one is born fully formed, and that we need not remain trapped by labels.

The name holds a second meaning for visitors to discover: V.O.A. also reads as voa, Portuguese for fly. Be free to fly.
