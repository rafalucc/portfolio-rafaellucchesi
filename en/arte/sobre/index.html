hello, universe

I was born in 1987 in Belo Horizonte, and I'm a self-taught visual artist. I learned on my own, experimenting and repeating until I found a visual language of my own.

In 2019, before the pandemic, that language began to take shape: Cubism, Hermeticism, comics, and urban art intersect in the paintings that carry my signature.

In 2021, that signature crossed the Atlantic. I was invited to a group exhibition at Cable Factory in Helsinki, Finland. In Brazil, my work has been shown at Centro Cultural dos Correios in Rio de Janeiro and Espaço Cultural dos Correios in Niterói.

Between 2023 and 2024, my work returned to Belo Horizonte for exhibitions at Museu MM Gerdau, Minas Tênis Clube II, and the Cobo and S/A galleries. “Beagá” isn't just where I was born: it's where my art continues to be made, revisited, and recognized, chapter after chapter.

Today, my work has crossed borders: I have collectors in Atlanta, Connecticut, and New York in the United States, with pieces also held in Libourne, France, and Puebla, Mexico.

For me, art is a matter of inheritance. I grew up in a family of artists, coloring my father Eduardo's drawings and my uncle Fernando's works when I was still a kid.

Maybe that's why I believe nothing in life happens by chance: everything has a reason, a motivation, a principle. Every action I take in the present answers a question I had already imagined before.
